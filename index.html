What We ENABLE

We help people understand indoor environments more clearly with technology built into everyday devices that interprets air signals responsibly and privately.

Device Integration
OutbreakSafe technology sits inside select devices, helping them recognise airborne biological signals in real time.
Cloud Interpretation
Connected devices can access secure cloud tools that analyse signal patterns and provide clear, easy‑to‑read environmental information.
Advanced Materials Science
Our platform is built on molecular technology designed to identify the presence of specific biological structures in the air.

Environmental Detection

Technology assisting devices to identify airborne biological signals forming the foundation of real‑time pathogen awareness.

Data Intelligence

Captured signals are interpreted through secure cloud frameworks, transforming raw detection into actionable environmental insights for partners multi sector applications.

Applied Integration

Collaboration with manufacturers to embed detection capability into everyday devices — expanding access to intelligent environmental monitoring worldwide.
